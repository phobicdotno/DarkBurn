# Changelog

## v0.0.23 (build 4)
- Draw lines, rectangles, ellipses and polygons
- Select shapes by click or box, then move, resize, rotate and shear them
- Snaps to the grid and to other shapes
- Shapes show in their layer colours, filled or as outlines
- Cuts / Layers list with a full cut settings editor
- Shape properties for the selected shapes
- Position and size fields that understand sums and units
- Colour palette puts shapes on a layer
- Save, open and recent projects
- Import SVG and pictures, also by dragging them in
- Undo, redo, copy, paste, group, align and arrange
- Auto-save and restore after a crash

## v0.0.11 (build 3)
- New Warning Label look: graphite with laser-sign yellow, now the default

## v0.0.10 (build 2)
- Dark and light themes, plus a pick of ready-made ones
- Its own window with a slim title bar
- All the menus are in place
- Toolbar with big buttons, grouped in tabs
- Side panels with tabs that can be moved, hidden and brought back
- Zoom and pan around the work area
- Rulers and a grid that gets finer as you zoom in
- Shows where the pointer is
- Remembers your layout and settings

## v0.0.1 (build 1)
- Project started
