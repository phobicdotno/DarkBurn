Status

Pre-release. DarkBurn has not yet burned a job on a real machine.

The machine drivers have run on the built-in simulators and loopback links only. If you connect a real laser, stay at the machine and keep its own stop button within reach. What is missing is listed under What is not there yet.

What it is

DarkBurn is a desktop program for laser cutters and engravers. You draw or import a design, give each layer a mode, a speed and a power, watch the preview, and send the job to the machine.

802 items on the parity checklist

  • 728 done
  • 48 partly done
  • 26 open

Most of them are what LightBurn 1.4.04 does; the rest are DarkBurn's own, such as the Gweike M3 Ultra and AI agents. It opens and saves LightBurn projects. DarkBurn is not affiliated with LightBurn Software.

Features

Everything in the README's feature lists is ticked as done on the parity checklist. What is partly done or missing is under What is not there yet.

  • 01

    Design and drawing

    Lines, curves, rectangles, ellipses and polygons on a canvas with rulers, a grid and snapping, placed by hand or by numbers.

  • 02

    Editing and arranging

    Node editing, boolean and offset tools, arrays, clean-up, align and distribute.

  • 03

    Text, barcodes and variable text

    Text with system and single-line fonts, text on a path, QR codes, barcodes, and text that changes from job to job.

  • 04

    Images and engraving

    Bring in pictures, adjust them, pick a dither mode and engrave, or trace them into cut lines.

  • 05

    Layers and cut settings

    What each layer does to the material: its mode, its speed and power, and everything under them.

  • 06

    Preview and planning

    See the job before it runs, with a time estimate, and decide the cut order and where the job lands.

  • 07

    Running a job

    Connect, frame, start, pause and stop, jog the head and talk to the controller.

  • 08

    Camera, rotary and print and cut

    The wizards and settings are done. The live camera parts are not (see What is not there yet).

  • 09

    Libraries

    A material library for cut settings and an art library for artwork you use again.

  • 10

    Interface, themes and languages

    One window with an app bar, a ribbon, tabbed panels and a status bar, with themes, remappable shortcuts and two languages.

  • 11

    Safety guards

    Warnings, checks and switches between you and an accidental start.

    None of these has been tried on a real machine. Most of them are checklist items.

  • The long lists

    Every group, bullet by bullet, is in the README.

Machines

Everything in this list ran on DarkBurn's own simulators or on loopback links. Nothing in it has run on a real machine.

  • GRBL family

    GRBL 1.1, older M3-only GRBL, GRBL-LPC, grblHAL and FluidNC, with auto-detect of xTool, Ortur, Atomstack and Sculpfun lasers.

  • Other G-code

    Smoothieware, Marlin and a custom G-code device.

    Limit LinuxCNC as file output only. Snapmaker is partly done.

  • Ruida

    Over Ethernet.

    Limit USB is partly done.

  • Galvo (EZCad2-style boards)

    Fibre, MOPA, UV and CO2 sources.

    Limit This build refuses a real galvo USB link.

  • Gweike M3 Ultra (fibre plus CO2 flatbed)

    Files for the vendor's own software.

    Limit DarkBurn does not connect to this machine.

  • Trocen / AWC, TopWisdom

    Not supported The protocols and file formats are not public.

  • Simulators

    A fresh install starts on the GRBL simulator. You can also add simulators for Ruida, Smoothieware, Marlin, Snapmaker, a custom G-code machine, a galvo board and the Gweike controller card, or a design-only device with no laser.

What each one can do, its limits, and connections
  • GRBL family. Streaming with buffer tracking, jogging, feed and power overrides while a job runs, a settings editor with backup, alarm and error codes in plain words, and a fire button.
  • Other G-code. LinuxCNC has no live link. Limits: Snapmaker is partly done. Snapmaker over the network, Emblaser 2 over Wi-Fi and Cubiio are listed in the wizard but wait for a capture from real hardware.
  • Ruida. Over Ethernet: start, pause, stop, frame, home, jog, send a file, the file list on the controller, machine settings with backup, controller info, dual tube, U axis and rotary. RD files are saved and imported. Limits: USB is partly done, and Fire needs Ethernet. Two boards (RDC6442S, RDC8445S) are recognised by card ID. Job start and end delays are missing.
  • Galvo. Lens correction, field calibration, frequency and Q-pulse, timing, wobble, red-light framing, repeat marking, drill mode and EZCad config import. Limits: door and foot switch inputs, the done-marking output, the rotary runner and 3D sliced images are partly done.
  • Gweike M3 Ultra. A DXF with one layer per material and the craft file (.chf), with fibre cut settings for assist gas, pierce stages and follower height. A simulator of the controller card plans a job and gives a time. Limits: nobody has yet checked that the exported files open in the vendor's software. The .nc export is partly done. The direct Ethernet link waits for a read-only capture on the real controller card. The safety item (the machine's height follower, piercing and gas logic stay in charge) stays partly done until there is a link to check it on.
  • Connections: USB serial with a baud choice, TCP/IP, UDP, Bluetooth serial, and a UDP automation socket for start and status. Raw USB, FTDI packet USB, HTTP and the TCP relay through a network bridge are partly done. "Find my laser" lists serial ports and USB devices but does not search the network.
  • Devices: a device list with import and export of profiles, a new device wizard, per-device work area, origin corner, start and end scripts and air assist commands, and a Machine Settings dialog with backup files.

Files

LightBurn .lbrn2 and older .lbrn projects open, and .lbrn2 can be saved back.

Projects
DarkBurn .dbrn projects hold embedded images, cut settings, planner settings, notes and a thumbnail.
Import
SVG, Adobe Illustrator, PDF with a page choice, DXF, PLT / HPGL, Ruida RD, and PNG, JPG, BMP, GIF, TIFF and WebP pictures. Several files at once, or by drag and drop.
Export
SVG, Adobe Illustrator and DXF, for the selection or for everything. PNG, JPG or BMP when the selection is a bitmap.
Machine files
G-code (.gc, .gcode, .nc, .txt) and Ruida RD. For the Gweike software: a DXF with one layer per material, and the craft file (.chf).
Beside the design
SHX single-line fonts, CSV and text files for variable text, and DarkBurn's own files for material and art libraries, device profiles, preferences, hotkeys, machine settings backups and camera calibration. Galvo lens correction files and EZCad configs are read.
Print
In black only or with colours, through a PDF handed to the system.
Not read or written
SignCut files, LightBurn library, art, device and hotkey files, Trocen UD5 and TopWisdom OUT files.

AI agents

darkburn-mcp ships next to the app. It is an MCP server that connects a client such as Claude Code or Claude Desktop to the running window, where every menu item, button and setting is also a command.

  • Off by default

    Off until you turn it on

    Nothing listens until you switch on "MCP control" in Settings > AI agents (MCP). The channel is on this computer only, with a new token for every session.

  • Off by default

    Machines are a separate switch

    The Gweike has one more switch of its own. Stop and pause are always allowed.

  • On by default

    Starting a job or firing asks you first

    A dialog with Confirm and Cancel and no keyboard default. This is the default, and you can switch it off.

  • Only you can change

    Agents cannot change these switches

    Every call goes into an audit log shown on the same settings page. A chip in the status bar shows connected agents and changes colour while machine control is allowed.

What an agent can do out of the box: draw and edit, set up layers, inspect the design and every cut setting, measure, take screenshots, and preview a job, G-code text included. A preview never sends anything.

Without a window: darkburn --mcp-headless serves the same tools on simulators only.

darkburn-mcp --print-config prints the lines that register it with Claude Code and Claude Desktop. On Windows the install folder is not on your PATH, so type the full path there: see first start.

The tools and the safety model are in crates/darkburn-mcp/README.md; the agent guide is darkburn-agent-guide.md.

Two things to know before you let an agent in.

File writes are allowed by default, and with them an agent can save or export to any path and overwrite what is there.

And the confirmation dialog and the window screenshot have not yet been checked by hand in the running app.

Install

No release is published yet, so there is no MSI to download yet, and both lines build DarkBurn from source.

Linux and macOS

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/phobicdotno/DarkBurn/main/scripts/install.sh | sh

On macOS DarkBurn builds and its tests pass on a build server, but nobody has used it on a Mac yet.

Windows, in PowerShell

irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/phobicdotno/DarkBurn/main/scripts/install.ps1 | iex

The Windows script has not been run on Windows yet; PowerShell 7.7 on Linux has run it against stand-ins for the Windows parts.

Building from source needs

  • Rust 1.95 or newer
  • A C toolchain
  • About 3.5 GB of disk

The first build took 13 minutes on a four-core computer.

Everything else (options, uninstall, what gets installed, building by hand): docs/INSTALL.md.

Releases: no package has been published.

What is not there yet

No job has been burned on a real machine, and the camera, rotary and print and cut parts have not met a real camera, a real rotary or a printed sheet.

  • 0 of 151 boxes ticked in the hardware check files
  • 48 checklist items partly done
  • 26 checklist items open

The end-to-end runs on the three target machines (an Atomstack on GRBL, a Ruida laser and the Gweike M3 Ultra) are open, and none of the 151 boxes in the hardware check files is ticked. The checks are done by hand: if you connect a real laser, you are doing one of them.

  • The hardware checksAnd the sister files for G-code machines, galvo boards and the Gweike controller card.
  • docs/BUGHUNT-NOTES.mdKnown open problems.
Partly done in the program
  • Camera: the camera picker with exposure and brightness, the live viewfinder, the capture settings, head-mounted camera stitching and trace from a camera capture.
  • Rotary: test rotation and the rotary jog arrows. Print and cut: setting the two targets.
  • Move panel: Focus Z and Set finish position.
  • Images: fast skip of blank areas in G-code, and the scanning offset table.
  • Cut settings: air assist with the DSP modes, and PPI mode.
  • Start here: a job can run from a point on G-code and DSP machines, but not on a galvo, and an RD file cannot be saved from a point.
  • Printing has no printer choice or page setup, and 1:1 scale depends on the PDF viewer.
  • Check for Updates works against a test server. The real request to the release feed has not been verified.
  • Toolbars can be shown, hidden and reordered, but the ribbon cannot split off, dock or float.
  • Shortcuts: F2 as a second Start key, the Z and diagonal jog keys, and Y and N in message boxes are missing. Jogging uses Ctrl+Alt+arrows instead of the numpad.
  • File dialogs are the system's own. "Ignore Start when the monitor is off" is stored but does nothing yet.
  • LightBurn projects: variable text state and virtual arrays are not carried over, and the bug hunt notes still ask for a check of bitmap and Y-axis orientation against a real file.
  • AI agents: imports and traces still run inside the call instead of reporting progress.
  • Nodes and devices: there is no separate node highlight tool, and the material library follows a device by its name while job origin and start-from stay with the project.
Open on the checklist
  • Languages other than English and Norwegian (22 are on the checklist), and translated machine error messages.
  • The Linux and macOS builds. Both build from source and the test suite passes on both on a build server. On macOS nobody has used the program yet.
  • Explorer thumbnails for project files and recent files in the Windows jump list.
  • SignCut import, and import of LightBurn library, art, device and hotkey files.
  • Newer features marked "later" on the checklist: tangent circle generator, true nest and quick nest, the Pages window, multi-file trace, 3D model to depth map, taper warp, continuous framing and a REST API.

What changed, version by version, is in CHANGELOG.md.
